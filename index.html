Home

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Institute for Analysis and Scientific Computing at TU Wien. I work in numerical analysis and scientific computing, focusing on uncertainty quantification, Model Order Reduction, boundary integral equations, and computational methods for wave propagation.

My research combines operator analysis and high-dimensional approximation with numerical methods for acoustic and electromagnetic scattering, time-dependent problems, and shape-parametric partial differential equations.

FWF Principal Investigator Project

Advancing UQ and MOR for Computational Electromagnetics

The project develops mathematically certified algorithms for shape-parametric uncertainty quantification and Model Order Reduction in computational electromagnetics. It combines dimension-robust approximation, Laplace-domain methods, high-frequency approximation, and neural-network surrogates.

PAT1779526 · €446,086 · TU Wien

National collaboration partner: Michael Feischl (TU Wien). International partners: Christoph Schwab (ETH Zürich) and Jürgen Dölz (University of Bonn).

FWF Research Radar

Before joining TU Wien in 2024, I was a postdoctoral researcher at EPFL in the Chair of Computational Mathematics and Simulation Science, working with Jan S. Hesthaven on Model Order Reduction for time-dependent problems.

More about me

I received my PhD in Applied and Computational Mathematics from ETH Zürich in 2021 under the supervision of Christoph Schwab. My dissertation, Shape Uncertainty Quantification in Acoustic Scattering, studied the analytic dependence of boundary integral operators and solutions on parametric domain deformations and its consequences for high-dimensional approximation.

Before my doctorate, I completed a Master II in Applied Mathematics at École Polytechnique and Université Pierre et Marie Curie, with a specialization in numerical analysis and partial differential equations. I also hold MSc and BSc degrees in Electrical Engineering from Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile.

Research interests

My research lies at the intersection of several areas in applied and computational mathematics:

  • Shape holomorphy and uncertainty quantification for acoustic and electromagnetic scattering.
  • Model Order Reduction for time-dependent problems using the Laplace transform.
  • Boundary and volume integral equations for wave propagation and inverse problems.
  • High-frequency approximation and neural-network surrogates with frequency-robust complexity.
  • Adaptive methods and reduced dynamics for parabolic problems and micromagnetics.
